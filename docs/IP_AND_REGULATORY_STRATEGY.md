# IP and Regulatory Strategy
**Status:** Not professional legal advice

## Core Question

Owner question from the conversation: **Is this something I should patent?**

## Suggested Middle Path

- File a provisional application now
- Keep building the product and gathering traction
- Convert to a utility filing if the traction supports it
- Keep implementation details as trade secrets where that is more valuable than disclosure

## Provisional Filing Fee Context

Per USPTO 37 CFR 1.16(d) basic provisional fees as reflected in the 2026-09-08 eCFR:

- Micro entity: $65
- Small entity: $130
- Large entity: $325

The approximate `$320` figure mentioned in the conversation corresponds to the large-entity fee only.

## Critical Public-Disclosure Constraint

Critical disclosure fact: `trademomentum-llc/heuristicmesh` is a public repository created 2026-08-29, and `docs/HeuristicMesh_Design_Spec.md` already published the four-framework architecture.

Any patent strategy must account for that disclosure. This document does not judge novelty or patentability.

## Regulatory and Product Positioning

- Transparency is the medical and safety moat because it supports FDA and CE explainability, clinical trust, and auditability.
- The prototype is not an FDA-cleared device.
- `heuristicmesh.com` is owned and aligns with the architectural philosophy.
- Jasterish is the mesh-logic language, not the product name.
