# Manufacturing BOM Summary (Target)

| Category              | Target Cost (qty 1,000) |
|-----------------------|-------------------------|
| Electronics           | $12 – 17                |
| Enclosure + hardware  | $2.00 – 3.00            |
| Packaging + manual    | $1.20                   |
| Test & programming    | $1.50                   |
| **Total COGS**        | **$17 – 23**            |

Retail positioning: $79 – $99 gives healthy margin even after channel costs.

### Recommended First Production
- 100–200 units for pilot homes
- Validate false-positive rate and mounting locations
- Then lock tooling for the enclosure
